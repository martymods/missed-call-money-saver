Enrollment steps

1
Authenticate. Your app issues a JWT for the user; add it to the URL or replace demo.jwt.token.
2
User applies. The embedded Unit flow collects KYC/KYB details and lets them pick debit options.
3
Provisioned instantly. Approved users see their Delco Tech account, card art, and balances.
4
Manage access. Invite additional users and roles by serving JWTs from your backend endpoint.

Production checklist

A
Production access. In the Unit dashboard go to your name/company → Production Access and request live keys, or email support@unit.co.
B
JWT generation. Replace demo.jwt.token with a JWT from your backend (e.g. Render) and pass it via ?token=<jwt> when loading /bank.
C
Embed on your domain. Deploy bank.html and /js/bank.js to delcotechdivision.com/bank; keep CSP allowing https://ui.s.unit.sh.
D
Render settings. Add UNIT_CLIENT_ID and UNIT_CLIENT_SECRET env vars; expose a /bank route that injects the JWT into the page response.
E
Order a card. After going live, open https://dashboard.unit.co → ensure Production is selected → find your customer → Issue Card (virtual or physical) and confirm shipping.
