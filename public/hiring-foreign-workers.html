2) Where we are actively hiring

Channels we use weekly to place drivers without cars and grill cooks.

  • NSC Philadelphia — primary immigrant workforce partner; evening shifts, ID-only onboarding, daily pay friendly.
  • Congreso de Latinos Unidos — connects Mexican, Honduran, Dominican, Guatemalan, and Salvadoran workers for kitchens and delivery.
  • JEVS Human Services — ESL, re-entry, and low-income candidates; many do not have vehicles.
  • Welcoming Center — broad immigrant job hub with cooks and couriers.
  • ACANA — West African community pipeline for grill and fire-cook talent.

Street & community sourcing

  • Vendor corridors: 52nd St, Hunting Park, Olney, Allegheny, Roosevelt Blvd, 69th St Terminal.
  • Latin markets: Mi Tienda Latina, El Pueblo Market, La Raza, Supermercado Mexico, 5th St / Rising Sun / Allegheny shops.
  • African barber shops: quick referrals for new arrivals needing work.
  • WhatsApp/Telegram: search “Empleos Philadelphia”, “Trabajos Philly”, “Drivers Philly”.
  • Displaced gig drivers: offer car + better pay than app rates for 4–10pm blocks.

Why Stripe Identity here?

  • Social ID verification: Stripe handles document + selfie; we never handle raw images.
  • Fast trust: candidates complete ID once; we re-use the verification session across assignments.
  • Borrowed controls: mirrors the credit-repair intake so compliance and storage stay consistent.
